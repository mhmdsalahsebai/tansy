# Code of conduct

Tansy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else
someone is acting on the project's behalf.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people, and accept
being told no. Harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, and publishing someone's private
information aren't tolerated.

## Reporting

Email mohamedsalahsebai@gmail.com with "tansy conduct" in the subject. Reports are read only by
the maintainer and kept confidential. If the report is about the maintainer, say so and it will be
handled by someone outside the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what happens, following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent one, depending on what happened
and whether it keeps happening.
